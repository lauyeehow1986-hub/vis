Formulas, Conventions & Primary Clinical References

Core VIS (Gaies 2010): VIS = Dopamine + Dobutamine + (100 × Adrenaline) + (100 × Noradrenaline base) + (10 × Milrinone) + (10,000 × Vasopressin in U/kg/min). Note: Noradrenaline (×100) is part of the original Gaies 2010 VIS definition (the older 1995 Wernovsky Inotrope Score omitted it).

Extended Adult VIS (Belletti 2021): Core VIS + (50 × Levosimendan) + (25 × Olprinone) + (20 × Methylene Blue in mg/kg/hr) + (10 × Phenylephrine) + (10 × Terlipressin in mcg/min) + (0.25 × Angiotensin II in ng/kg/min) + (1 × Enoximone). Note: Olprinone and Enoximone are greyed out because they are not registered in Singapore's HSA Register of Therapeutic Products or National Drug Formulary (NDF).
